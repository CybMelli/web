About Glow Beauty

Our Story

Founded in 2020, Glow Beauty was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves to feel beautiful in their own skin. We're a team of makeup enthusiasts, chemists, and artists dedicated to creating products that enhance your natural beauty.

We source the finest ingredients from around the world and formulate our products with love, care, and scientific precision. Every product is tested on humans (never animals) to ensure safety, performance, and comfort.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your return policy? ▼
We offer a 30-day return policy for all unused products. Simply contact our support team and we'll guide you through the process. Return shipping is free!
Are your products cruelty-free? ▼
Yes! All Glow Beauty products are 100% cruelty-free. We never test on animals and only partner with suppliers who share our ethical standards.
How long does shipping take? ▼
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping (1-2 days) is available for an additional fee. We ship worldwide!
Do you offer international shipping? ▼
Yes, we ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping typically takes 7-14 business days. Customs fees may apply depending on your location.
Are your products safe for sensitive skin? ▼
All our products are dermatologist-tested and formulated with gentle, non-irritating ingredients. We avoid common irritants and offer a range of hypoallergenic options.
How can I track my order? ▼
Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can also track your order in your account dashboard.
